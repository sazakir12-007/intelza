# Intelza teacher's guide

**INTELZA · Understanding Beyond Answers**

Intelza checks how well your class understood a topic. Every student holds up a printed
answer card; you scan the room with your phone and see the results at once.

Open the menu with **☰** (top left) or by swiping in from the left edge. It lists every
part of the app: your classes, subjects and topics, reports, tools and settings.

## 1. Set up once

1. **Add a class.** Menu → Classes → **New class**, e.g. "Grade 3 – B".
2. **Add students.** In the class, tap **Add student**, or use ⋮ → **Import students** to
   load a list saved as CSV from a spreadsheet (names in the first column; optional roll
   number and card number columns). Every student gets a card number.
3. **Print the cards.** In the class, tap the printer icon. Choose 2 cards per page
   (standard) or 1 per page (large, for big rooms), then **Print** or **Share PDF**.
   - Print in black on white, on thick matte paper or card. Don't shrink the page.
   - Without names, one numbered set (Menu → Tools → Print a numbered set) works for
     every class, because card numbers match each class's list.
4. **Check the cards.** Menu → Tools → **Test cards**. Point the camera at a card and turn
   it: the letter shown should follow the edge that is on top.

Want to try the app first? Settings → **Sample data** adds two test classes (Grade 2 and
Grade 3, 10 students each) and 160 ready-made questions. Remove them there when done.

## 2. Prepare questions (optional)

Menu → Question bank → a subject → **New topic** → **Add question**.

- **Multiple choice**: 2–4 options; select the correct one.
- **True/False**: card A means True, card B means False.
- **Poll**: no right answer (e.g. "How well did you understand?"); never scored.
- Add a picture from the gallery or camera if it helps.

No time to prepare? Use **Quick questions** when starting an evaluation and ask out loud.

## 3. Run an evaluation

1. **Start evaluation** (Home or the menu), pick the class and the topic.
2. Show or read the question. With a TV connected, the question appears there; otherwise
   tap the presenter icon to show it full screen (e.g. while screen-mirroring).
3. Students turn their card so their answer's letter is **on top** and hold it up,
   facing you.
4. Tap **Scan answers** and slowly sweep the camera across the room. Each card turns
   green when its answer is recorded. Watch the "18 of 25 answered" counter and the list
   of students still missing.
5. Tap **Done** to see the results: % correct, a chart and who chose what. Tap a name to
   correct an answer by hand. On the TV, results stay hidden until you tap **Reveal**.
6. **Next question**, and at the end **Finish evaluation** to open the report.

You can leave and come back later: unfinished evaluations appear on Home under
**Continue evaluation**. Use the attendance icon to mark absent students.

### Scanning tips

- Good light helps; avoid strong light behind the students.
- Students should hold cards still, flat towards you, without fingers over the black
  square.
- From the back of a big room, use the **2×** or **3×** zoom buttons, or print large
  cards.
- Answers are read correctly whether you hold the phone upright or sideways.

## 4. Reports

- **Session report**: class average, students who need help, questions to re-teach,
  every student's score and every question's results. Share as PDF or CSV (spreadsheet).
- **Student report**: scores over time and averages per subject.
- **Topic report**: how a topic went in every class.
- **Class report**: progress over time, lowest averages first, and a gradebook CSV.

The "needs help" and "re-teach" thresholds can be changed in Settings.

## 5. Your data

Everything stays on your phone. Intelza has no account and no internet access.

To move to a new phone: Settings → **Back up** (save or share the file), then on the new
phone Settings → **Restore**.

## 6. Appearance

Settings → Appearance: System, Light or Dark theme, and the accent colour. The theme can
also be switched at the bottom of the menu.
